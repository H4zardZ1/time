I sense the chaos that once was my whole world fleeting.

...

I sense a new, alien world. Black. Empty. I must brace myself.

...

Who am I? I do not remember anything but the weight of a whole reality, projected to my mind at once, eternally.

...

All that numbing sense of everything is now nothing.

...

I am here, thinking. I feel... like I want to see what happens next, if I think hard enough.
